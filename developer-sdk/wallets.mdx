A Swig wallet gives each authority a role with specific permissions. Your server
prepares an operation, the appropriate authority signs it, and your application
submits it and checks confirmation.

## Policies, roles, and signing

- A **policy template** is saved creation configuration in the Developer Portal.
  Reading or editing a template does not change an existing wallet's roles.
- A **role** binds an authority to permissions on the wallet. Choose the minimum
  actions and limits that authority needs.
- An **API key** authenticates hosted requests. The wallet authority separately
  authorizes the prepared operation through a signature or approval proofs.
- A **signer** belongs to your application. Keep private keys in the runtime
  responsible for signing.

## Follow the transaction lifecycle

1. Read the state needed to choose the wallet, role, and operation.
2. Prepare the unsigned transaction with the Developer SDK.
3. Inspect the prepared operation and collect the required signatures.
4. [Submit or sponsor](/developer-sdk/sponsor-and-submit) the signed transaction.
5. Check chain confirmation, then read the resulting wallet state.

Submission acceptance is separate from chain confirmation. Keep confirmation
tracking in your application, and follow the operation's idempotency rules when
retrying.

For holdings and activity, use [Query](/query). For funding, transfers, swaps,
staking, and sponsorship, use [Finance](/developer-sdk/finance).
