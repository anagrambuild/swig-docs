Choose a flow by what should happen to the funds. Wallet permissions and
application-owned signing still apply to operations that move assets.

## Choose a flow

| Goal | Guide | What to expect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Buy crypto with fiat or sell crypto for fiat | [Ramping](/developer-sdk/ramp) | Discover supported routes, request a quote, create an order, and follow its status |
| Send SOL or tokens | [Transfers](/developer-sdk/transfers-and-swaps) | Prepare a transfer for an authorized wallet role to sign |
| Exchange assets or combine operations | [Swaps](/developer-sdk/swaps-and-batches) | Prepare a swap or grouped wallet operations |
| Stake SOL and manage the position | [Yield](/developer-sdk/yield) | Choose a validator, deposit, deactivate, and withdraw when the stake is available |
| Pay transaction fees for users | [Paymaster](/examples/dev-portal/paymaster-overview) | Fund a paymaster, configure limits, and sponsor application-signed transactions |
| Pay for an HTTP resource | [x402 payments](/developer-sdk/x402) | Read a payment requirement, prepare and sign a payment, then retry the resource request |

## Understand the costs

- **Hosted API usage:** metered requests share your organization's allowance.
  See [pricing and quotas](/query#pricing-and-included-usage).
- **Transaction fees:** the transaction's fee payer or paymaster funds chain
  execution. Keep the paymaster funded for the network you use.
- **Ramp and swap quotes:** inspect amounts, provider fees, and slippage in the
  selected flow before collecting authorization.
- **Staking:** validator rates and reward estimates are estimates. Native stake
  activation and deactivation affect when funds can earn rewards or be withdrawn.

## Track the result

Use the identifier returned by each flow to follow its outcome. A submitted
transaction needs chain confirmation; a ramp order has its own status lifecycle.

- Check [wallet balances](/query/balances) and [token movements](/query/wallets).
- Inspect [DEX activity](/query/dex) and [yield positions and rewards](/query/yield).
- Follow [ramp orders](/query/ramp) and monitor [paymaster balances](/query/paymasters).

Indexed query results can lag chain state. Use the freshness signals described
by each query guide when showing results to users.
